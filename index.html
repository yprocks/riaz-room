Voice type here is a guess from pitch only: where your held, comfortable notes sit. It skips speech and slides, keeps learning across sessions, and slowly forgets old singing so it follows you if your voice changes. A teacher also listens to tone colour and where your voice flips, so treat it as a pointer.
